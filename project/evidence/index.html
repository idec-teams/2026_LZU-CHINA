    03 / Follow the evidence

    What we know. What remains open.¶

    A good research story should let you inspect the reasoning behind it. Start here to follow observations back to their figures and records.

    Reading our evidence labels¶

    • Experimental observation: a measurement or outcome from our study, linked to its figure where available.
    • Documented activity: community work presented with its photographs, activity records or materials.
    • Proposed direction: an idea or development goal that has not been demonstrated by our current evidence.

    Trace a claim in three steps¶

    1. Read the observation and its limit in Experimental results.
    2. Follow the corresponding figure through the Engineering record.
    3. Check the methods and data availability.

    Community evidence follows the same principle¶

    Our education, collaboration and inclusivity pages connect activity descriptions to complete records. Attendance figures describe reach; they are not, by themselves, proof of learning. Feedback describes what participants reported; it is not a controlled estimate of effectiveness.

    Why the open questions stay visible¶

    Our public research record remains incomplete. We preserve original figures and identify where further records are needed rather than filling gaps with reconstructed data. The responsible research page and attribution statement explain the scope and accountability behind this wiki.
