# Engineering record

<p class="page-deck">Our engineering index connects eleven DBTL cycles with the associated figures and outstanding documentation.</p>

We organised our engineering work into eleven Design–Build–Test–Learn cycles. This retrospective index links each topic to our observations and figures. It is **not a dated laboratory notebook**: experiment dates, operator names, plate maps, raw values and instrument files are not yet available in our public research record.

| Cycle | Engineering focus | Observations and documentation status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Culture conditions and preparation of *Salmonella* culture filtrate | We recorded growth in LB to OD600 above 1.2 and no colonies on the filtrate sterility plate after 48 h. The original plate image and count sheet remain unavailable for verification. |
| 2 | EcN bile-salt tolerance | We observed no apparent growth inhibition up to 5% sodium cholate and selected 0.2% for routine culture. The underlying data and plotted figure remain unavailable for verification. |
| 3 | EcN transformation and reporter compatibility | We compared CaCl2, RbCl and electroporation. The electroporation estimate of >10^7 CFU/µg DNA remains to be verified against clone-level data. |
| 4 | Sequence acquisition and codon optimization | Our sequence summary lists CAI >0.85, GC 48–54% and 100% Sanger identity. FASTA files and chromatograms remain unavailable for verification. |
| 5 | Culture-filtrate response associated with AI-2 | We recorded an approximately 4.8-fold response at 5–10% filtrate. The underlying curve and raw measurements remain unavailable for verification. |
| 6 | Tetrathionate tolerance and PttrB-associated reporter expression | Fig. 3; expression peaked at 0.6‰; growth decreased at 0.8–1.0‰. |
| 7 | Cross-induction matrix | Fig. 4A; methods count (21) conflicts with the plotted 7 × 4 design (28). |
| 8 | LacZ/X-Gal colorimetric output | Fig. 4A; assay timing was 12 h plus 2 h. |
| 9 | *Salmonella* biofilm assay | Fig. 4B and OD595 values. |
