AI-assisted writing and illustrative ideas are distinguished from measured results. The editing process does not create experiment records, participant responses or evidence of completed directed evolution. Our disclosure follows the [iDEC AI policy](https://idec.io/pages/aipolicy.html); the final report should also identify substantial AI use and the review performed.

The GutSentry mark and signal-atlas illustration are original SVG artwork created with AI-assisted coding for this website. They are conceptual illustrations, not microscopy or experimental images. The learning lab uses deliberately invented scores to explain how evaluation criteria affect a ranking; these scores are not project data. The interactive AND gate explains an idealised logical rule and is not a fitted biological model.

Four AI-generated cartoon illustrations accompany the project story, learning lab, education and inclusivity introductions.
