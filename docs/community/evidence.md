# Make the contribution inspectable

A record should show who needed help, what was provided, what was used and what changed. This page distinguishes prepared resources from verified community outcomes.

| Contribution area | Current evidence | Remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Evidence to add after real use

For each contribution, record a date, resource version, partner context, the expressed need, the team’s contribution, evidence of use, feedback and the resulting change. Include a partner-confirmed account where available and permitted. Separate distinct partner teams from repeat interactions.

## Claims we do not make

We do not claim a completed iDEC network, formal organiser partnership, verified adoption, fulfilled mentoring commitment or demonstrated learning gain from the new toolkit. No such evidence has yet been supplied for this initiative.
