# A resource becomes a contribution when someone can use it.

<p class="lead">Learn together. Review each other’s explanations. Leave something useful for the next team.</p>

Our community initiative brings the LZU-CHINA education and exchange experience into reusable resources for directed-evolution learners. This is a team initiative, not an official iDEC programme.

## What counts as a useful contribution?

A download or attendance count shows reach. A partner’s account of using a resource shows use. A documented revision with a follow-up check can show how the work improved. These are different claims and require different evidence.

We propose recording **need → contribution → use → feedback → revision → follow-up**. This is our evidence framework, not an official scoring system.

## Contribute without adding barriers

The kit works offline and does not require laboratory access. English and Chinese instructions are provided. Letters and words accompany colour cues. Resources may be adapted under CC BY 4.0 with credit; existing third-party materials retain their own terms.

To propose a correction, use the [repository issue page](https://github.com/idec-teams/2026_LZU-CHINA/issues) or include the completed template in an agreed exchange. This website does not collect submissions or send messages automatically. No response time is promised until a maintainer is assigned.
