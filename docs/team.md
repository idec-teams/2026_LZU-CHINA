# Meet the LZU-CHINA community

<p class="page-deck">A community of 68 people across research leadership, mentorship, student coordination and advisory groups. Open a portrait to meet the person.</p>

Our 68-person LZU-CHINA community brings together principal investigators, instructors, students and advisors. This directory introduces the wider community behind our project and presents our shared interest in learning, collaboration and responsible biological innovation. Role groups reflect the supplied team directory; formal competition participation is recorded separately.
