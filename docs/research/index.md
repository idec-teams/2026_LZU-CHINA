## Engineering and evolution

Our work currently covers engineering design and in-vitro characterisation. We have not established a complete directed-evolution campaign with variant lineages, selection rounds and ancestor comparisons. The [evolution perspective](../project/evolution.md) explains the distinction; the [learning lab](../learn/index.md) offers an abstract teaching exercise.
