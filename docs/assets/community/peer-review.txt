4. What is proposed, observed or still unknown? 区分设想、观测与未知。
5. Could a newcomer use the resource without help? 新手是否能独立使用？
6. Check keyboard use, colour-independent meaning, readable text and mobile layout. 检查键盘、非颜色提示、文字与手机阅读。
